How it works

From pantry chaos to a one-tap reorder

Five small features that quietly keep your kitchen stocked.

📦

Automatic low-stock detection

Set a minimum per item and the dashboard instantly flags anything that drops below it. No math, no mental tracking.

✨

AI-powered category & unit suggestions

Type a name, click "Suggest details", and Gemini fills in the category, unit, and a sensible minimum for you.

🛒

One-click reorder list

Add any low-stock item to a single reorder list in one tap. Remove items as you go — the list stays in sync.

💬

Ready-to-copy WhatsApp-style message

The app generates a clean, friendly reorder message you can paste into WhatsApp, SMS, or email. One button, no typing.

⚡

Works fully in your browser

No installation, no signup, no servers. Just open the page and start tracking. Your data stays on your device.
